学习路径Learning path · 第 5 阶段Stage 5

先发极简版

Ship a rough version first

**这一步决定什么****What this stage decides**：游戏行业的最大风险不是「做得不好玩」，而是「你不知道它好不好玩」。: The biggest risk in this industry is not \"the game is bad\" — it is \"you do not know whether it is bad\".

**本站的判断****Our judgement**：在 itch.io 上传一个能玩的、素材很粗糙的版本 —— AI 让这变得可行。: Upload something playable on itch.io with deliberately crude assets — AI is what makes this affordable.

性质：本站的判断，未经实测Nature: the site's judgement, not measured

一句话：绝大多数独立游戏的失败原因不是不好玩，而是做完 80% 才发现方向错了。

In one line: most indie games do not fail because they were bad — they fail because the wrong direction was discovered after 80% was done.

⚠ 行业最大的风险

你以为的风险真实风险
做得不够好⚠ 方向错了 —— 做得多好都没用
竞争太激烈⚠ 没人玩（但没人知道，因为没人试过）

⚠ 你无法通过「更努力地做」来发现方向错误 ——

因为你是在做完之后才知道，而那时已经投入了几百小时。

⚠ The biggest risk in the industry

The risk you think it isThe real risk
Not polished enough⚠ The direction is wrong — no amount of polish saves it
Too much competition⚠ Nobody plays it (and nobody knows, because nobody tried)

⚠ You cannot discover a wrong direction by working harder on it —

because you find out after finishing, by which point you have invested hundreds of hours.

为什么 AI 让这件事可行了

过去：发一个能玩的版本 = 3~6 个月（美术、音频、关卡都要做完）

现在：⚠ 原型成本降到了可以「多发几个试」的程度

⚠ 这才是 AI 做游戏真正带来的方法论变化 ——

不是「做得更快」，而是「试错的次数变多了」。

Why AI makes this affordable

Before: shipping something playable took 3–6 months (art, audio and levels all had to be finished)

Now: ⚠ prototypes are cheap enough to "ship a few and try"

⚠ This is the real methodological change AI brings to game-making —

not "it gets done faster", but "you get more attempts in".

怎么做

1. 砍到只剩核心

发出去的版本必须有：能操作、有目标、有反馈。

⚠ 不能有：好看的美术、完整关卡、设置菜单、音效。

2. ⚠ 明确说清这是「试玩版」

⚠ 不要让人以为这是最终版 —— 否则负面反馈会盖过真实反馈。

3. 看数据，不看留言

信号说明什么
多少人玩完了⚠ 核心循环是否成立
⚠ 有多少人玩第二次⚠ 这是最关键的指标 —— 没人玩第二次说明不够有趣
留言里问的是什么「怎么玩」= 引导问题；「还有吗」= 需求真实

How to do it

1. Cut it down to the core

What ships must have: it is operable, goal-directed and responsive.

⚠ What must not ship: nice art, complete levels, a settings menu, sound effects.

2. ⚠ Say clearly that it is a playtest

⚠ Do not let people think it is the final version — otherwise negative feedback drowns out the real signal.

3. Read the numbers, not the comments

SignalWhat it tells you
How many finished it⚠ Whether the core loop holds
⚠ How many played a second time⚠ The most important metric — nobody replaying means it is not interesting enough
What the comments ask"How do I play this?" = onboarding problem; "Is there more?" = real demand

常见失败

  • ⚠ 追求「做完再发」 —— 最常见的失败模式，也最难自己察觉
  • 发给朋友 —— ⚠ 朋友会给你礼貌性反馈，陌生人才给你真实反馈
  • 看点赞数 —— ⚠ 点赞是礼貌，不是参与

Common failures

  • ⚠ Aiming to finish before shipping — the most common failure mode, and the hardest to notice yourself
  • Sending it to friends — ⚠ friends give you polite feedback; strangers give you real feedback
  • Watching the like count — ⚠ a like is politeness, not participation
